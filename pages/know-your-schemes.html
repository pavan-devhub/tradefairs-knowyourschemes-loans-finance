Schemes for Central Government Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Explore the available schemes, view details, eligibility criteria and apply.

  • PM-RKVY (PM Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana)

    50-70% infra; 25% production; Rs.25L startup seed

    Grant/Infrastructure

  • AIF — Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

    3% interest subvention; net ~5-7%; up to Rs.200L per project location; unlimited locations for FPOs/coops

    Loan/Subsidy

  • 10,000 FPO Scheme — Equity Grant + H&CB

    Rs.25L non-repayable equity/FPO (SFAC); Rs.15L/yr × 3 yrs H&CB = Rs.70L total per FPO

    Equity/Grant

  • MIDH — Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture

    General: 40-50% back-ended; SC/ST/Women/NE: 60%; Polyhouse Rs.710/sqm benchmark; Packhouse 40% up to Rs.250L project

    Subsidy

  • PMKSY-PDMC — Per Drop More Crop (MIF)

    Centre 55% general; 60% SC/ST/Women; +20-25% AP top-up = EFFECTIVE 75-85%

    Subsidy

  • SMAM — Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation

    FPO/SHG/Coop Farm Machinery Bank: 80% (Revised 2025); Individual SC/ST: 50%; General: 40%; CHC: up to Rs.1.5 Cr

    Subsidy

  • Namo Drone Didi — SHG Women Drone Enterprise

    80% subsidy on Rs.8L drone; farmer pays Rs.1.6L; LFC mandatory channel; Rs.15K-25K/drone/yr income

    Grant/Infrastructure

  • PKVY — Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (Organic Farming)

    Rs.50K/ha over 3 yrs: Rs.31K inputs+Rs.8.8K cert+Rs.10.2K farm dev; 60% infra grant on cluster

    Grant

  • PM-KISAN — PM Kisan Samman Nidhi

    Rs.6000/yr (Rs.2000 × 3 instalments); direct DBT; auto after enrollment

    DBT

  • AgriStack / Digital Agriculture Mission

    Free Farmer ID; free DBT linkage; KCC in 30 min with AgriStack; MY FARM pre-populated

    Infrastructure/Certification
